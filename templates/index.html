Why EcoQuest?

Traditional environmental education is often theoretical and fails to engage students meaningfully. EcoQuest addresses this by combining structured lessons with gamification — XP, levels, badges, streaks and leaderboards — so students stay motivated to learn and act on sustainability topics, while teachers get a simple digital tool to support classroom learning.
